# Health, Nutrition & Activity Tracking Patterns

## Patterns Extracted from Macrometric, Macros-Calculator, Akilo, Tonnd
1. **Macro Ring / Radial Progress**: 3-tier concentric radial bars for Protein, Carbs, and Fats with caloric budget target center display.
2. **Metric Summary Tiles**: Compact cards showing Calories Remaining, Water Intake, Steps Count, Active Energy with delta indicator vs 7-day average.
3. **Timeline Meal Log**: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack chronological cards with quick-add actions and macro breakdown badge pills.
4. **Empty State Food Log**: Engaging zero-data state with illustrated plate, macro targets, and "Log Your First Meal" primary CTA.
